Fox Davidson is a whole of market UK mortgage and property finance broker, directly authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. We arrange residential, large and high net worth, professional and complex income, and buy-to-let mortgages, together with bridging loans, commercial and semi-commercial mortgages and property development finance, for high earners, high net worth individuals, landlords and developers. Loans from £250,000; large and high net worth mortgages from £1,000,000.

# Regulated scope

Regulated bridging is arranged where the main security is a first charge; a second charge on another property can sit behind it as supplementary security. Fox Davidson does not arrange standalone regulated second charge mortgages or lifetime mortgages.
